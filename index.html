About Us

We provide innovative property and land surveying, development, management, and consulting solutions within the Estate management industry. As industry leaders, we employ cutting-edge strategies to address pressing needs. Our commitment to timely service delivery and turnkey management sets us apart. We tailor our services to each client's unique needs, handling projects from their perspective.

Our Services

Property Valuation

We offer property valuation based on location, condition, and expert assessment. Our experienced surveyors consider factors like land, age, size, and market trends for accurate value estimation.

Property Development

We offer comprehensive project solutions—pre-project, implementation, post-commissioning. Experienced in property development, we manage land, design, approvals, construction, management, and sales for your success.

Land Survey

As a key player in land development, we specialize in precise measuring and mapping of construction sites. Our skilled engineers leverage this data to create safe and effective designs, capitalizing on the landscape's uniqueness.

Property Management

We provide comprehensive management solutions for residential and commercial properties. Our team handles maintenance, tenant relations, rent collection, and property optimization to maximize your investment returns.

Estate Survey

We provide expert advice on optimal land use and property management. Serving landowners' interests, we devise infrastructure development plans. With expertise in estate management, we handle property maintenance, valuations, and enhance client benefits.

Consultancy

We offer professional expertise, advice, services and opinions to people of different classes in the society based on their individual real estate needs and wants.

Oil and Gas Consulting

We offer expertise to professionals in upstream, midstream, and downstream sectors. We advise on asset management, acquisition, partnerships, international agreements, financing, and more.

Acquisition Compensation

We perform land and property surveys for project-related land acquisition. Our services also include assessing the value of properties for open market sales on behalf of clients.

Installation and Commissioning

We specialize in construction, commissioning, and installation services. We excel in project management, offering EPC or EPCM approaches for integrated engineering, procurement, construction, and management.
